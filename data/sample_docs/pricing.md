# Northwind Cloud Pricing

Northwind Cloud offers three plans.

## Starter
Starter costs $29 per month and includes 5 projects and 100 GB of egress per month.

## Pro
Pro costs $99 per month and includes 50 projects and 1 TB of egress per month.

## Enterprise
Enterprise is priced by custom quote and includes unlimited projects and negotiated egress.

## Billing
Annual billing gives a 20% discount on Starter and Pro. Egress beyond the included amount is billed at $0.08 per GB on Starter and Pro.

Every new account gets a 14-day free trial. No credit card is required to start the trial.
