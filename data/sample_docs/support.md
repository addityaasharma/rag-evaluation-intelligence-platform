# Northwind Cloud Support and SLAs

## Support tiers
- Starter: email support with a response within 2 business days.
- Pro: email and chat support with a response within 8 business hours.
- Enterprise: 24/7 support with a 30-minute response for priority 1 incidents.

## Uptime SLA
- Starter: no uptime SLA.
- Pro: 99.9% monthly uptime. If uptime falls below this, Pro customers receive a 10% service credit.
- Enterprise: 99.99% monthly uptime.

Contact support at support@northwind.example.
