# Northwind Cloud Security and Data Handling

## Encryption
Data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3.

## Backups and deletion
Backups are retained for 35 days. When a project is deleted, its data is purged within 30 days.

## Compliance
Northwind Cloud holds SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certifications.

## Regions
Data can be pinned to one of three regions: eu-west, us-east or ap-south.

## Single sign-on
SAML single sign-on is available on the Pro and Enterprise plans only.
